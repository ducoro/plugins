# Seasonal & Marketing Anchor Calendar

Use seasonal anchors to pull evergreen ideas into timely distribution windows. Prepare assets `leadDays` before each date so Gate 2 review never happens at the last minute.

| Date / Window | Anchor | Region | Lead Days | Strong Angle Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `01-01` | New Year / Annual Reset | Global | 7–10 | Tooling stacks, annual checklists, lessons from last year |
| Jan–Feb (Lunar) | Spring Festival / Year-End Summary | CN | 14 | Year-end teardown, holiday automation, return-to-work guides |
| `02-14` | Valentine's Day | Global | 7 | Gifting, pairing guides, light relatable stories |
| `03-08` | International Women's Day | Global / CN | 7–10 | Creator/founder spotlights, real practical guides |
| `05-01` | Labor Day Holiday | CN | 10 | Travel/productivity setups, binge-worthy deep dives |
| `06-18` | 618 Mid-Year Shopping | CN | 14 | Honest product comparisons, avoid-pitfall buying guides |
| `09-01` | Back-to-School / Q4 Planning | Global / CN | 10 | Workflow resets, study/work templates, Q4 roadmaps |
| `10-01` | National Day Golden Week | CN | 14 | Long-form tutorials, curated resource packs |
| `11-11` | Double 11 Shopping Festival | CN | 14 | Side-by-side teardowns, ROI calculators, real user tests |
| Late Nov | Black Friday / Cyber Week | Global | 14 | Deal roundups, annual stack audits, transparent pricing comparisons |
| `12-25` – `12-31` | Year-End Wrap & Next-Year Bets | Global | 10 | Top-performing lessons, honest metrics recap, next-year predictions |

## Industry-Specific Anchors
Add domain-specific dates (major conferences, quarterly earnings, flagship OS/model release seasons, tax deadlines) directly to `brand-profile.md` so the daily intake loop flags them automatically.
